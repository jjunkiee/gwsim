# T1.5.1 DSL coverage study of the 72 M1 skills

**Task:** T1.5.1 (P1, WP1.5) · **Type:** Research · **Date:** 2026-09-22 · **Owner approved the 72-page fetch**

**Question:** can the effect DSL in §8.4 express the 72 M1 skills, and what does it lack?

**Method:** all 72 wiki pages were fetched, three seconds apart, with an honest User-Agent and
article URLs only. None returned 403 or 429. Each skill was then written in pseudo-DSL using
only §8.4's constructs, and anything that could not be written became a gap.

**Nothing in this file paraphrases a skill description.** The pseudo-DSL is our own encoding
and the classifications are our own judgements. The cached pages stay in `.cache/`, which is
git-ignored (EXT-5, C2).

---

## 1. The headline finding: the design over-estimates handlers

§8.5 names **16** M1 skills as likely handlers. Having written all 72 out, **four** genuinely
need Rust:

| Skill | Why the DSL cannot express it |
| --- | --- |
| **Arcane Echo** | It replaces *itself* on the bar with whatever spell is cast next, for a duration. That is a change to the skill bar's own contents, which no declarative effect can describe. |
| **Air of Superiority** | It grants a *random* benefit drawn from the Asura set on each experience-granting kill. The DSL has no construct for "pick one of a named set", and adding one for a single skill would be worse than a handler. |
| **Soul Twisting** | It changes the *cost and recharge* of a whole skill category, counts uses, and ends after N of them. Cost modification scoped by skill type, with a consumption counter, is three separate mechanisms that only interact here. |
| **Master of Magic** | It **sets** four attributes to a value rather than adding to them, and changes energy returned per spell. The set-rather-than-add is expressible (§3); the interaction with attunements is not. |

**The other twelve are expressible**, given the additions in §3. Working through them one by
one is what changed the estimate:

- **Panic** and **Mistrust** are hexes carrying a *trigger*: "when the hexed foe does X, do Y".
  §8.4 already has `Triggered(event, filter, actions, charges)`. Both fit it directly.
- **Blood Bond** and **Putrid Bile** are hexes with two triggers each, one on being hit and one
  on death. Again `Triggered`, twice.
- **Shelter**, **Union**, **Displacement**, **Life** and **Recuperation** are binding rituals
  that create a spirit with an aura. `CreateSpirit` exists; what was missing is a way to say
  "the spirit itself takes damage when its aura fires", which is a trigger on the spirit.
- **Animate Bone Fiend** is `Summon`, which §8.4 already lists.
- **Flesh of My Flesh** and **Resurrection Chant** are `Resurrect`, which §8.4 already lists.
- **Splinter Weapon** is a weapon spell with charges — `Triggered` with `charges`.
- **Protective Was Kaolai** needs bundle actions (§3), but is otherwise ordinary.

**Why this matters.** §22 lists encoding volume as the project's largest risk, at ~1,400
skills with ~320 handlers. If the handler rate over a real 72-skill sample is **4 rather than
16**, the design's ~23% estimate is closer to **6%**, and the projected handler count drops
from ~320 to something near 80. That is a different project. **One sample is not proof** — M1
is Mesmer-heavy and the Kournan roster is ordinary — but it is strong enough to be worth
re-testing at the next profession batch rather than planning around 320.

## 2. Classification of all 72

| Class | Count | Share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Formulaic** — values applied with no condition or trigger | 19 | 26% |
| **Conditional** — an `If`, a filter, or a `Triggered` | 49 | 68% |
| **Handler** | 4 | 6% |

§8.4's sample predicted 20% / 57% / 23%. Formulaic and conditional are close; handlers are
not.

**Formulaic (19):** Executioner's Strike · Sprint · Troll Unguent · Remove Hex · Blood is
Power · Fireball · Meteor · Life Siphon · Recuperation · Shatter Enchantment · "Stand Your
Ground!" · "Incoming!" · Armor of Unfeeling · Spirit Transfer · Spirit Siphon · Signet of
Spirits · Union · Resurrection Chant · Flesh of My Flesh

**Handler (4):** Arcane Echo · Air of Superiority · Soul Twisting · Master of Magic

**Conditional (49):** everything else.

**The 31 Kournan skills are classified here for the first time**, as §20.3 asked. They split
17 conditional, 13 formulaic, 1 handler (Master of Magic). They are *less* exotic than the
party's skills, which makes sense: they are ordinary foe bars rather than a curated meta team.

## 3. Constructs §8.4 lacks

Each row is something at least one M1 skill needs and §8.4 cannot express.

### Values

| Construct | Needed by | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `PercentOf(quantity)` | Aura of Restoration, Spirit Siphon, Flesh of My Flesh, Master of Magic | "200…500% of the energy cost", "30…60% of that energy", "half your health". §8.4 has `Percent(…)` but nothing that names *what* the percentage is of. |
| `Capped(value, max)` | Reversal of Fortune, Signet of Creation | Both state an explicit maximum. `Min` exists and covers it, but the intent reads better named. |
| `Floored(value, min)` | Soul Twisting | "15 less, minimum 5". |
| `PerUnit` **extensions** | Energy Surge, Spirit Transfer, Life, Convert Hexes, Cautery Signet, Signet of Creation | The construct exists; the *quantities* it counts do not. Needed: energy lost, health lost, seconds elapsed, hexes removed, conditions removed, creatures controlled. |

### Selectors and filters

| Construct | Needed by | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Nearest(selector)` | Spirit Siphon, Spirit Transfer | "the spirit nearest you". §8.4 has `Spirits` but no way to pick one. |
| `Owned` filter | Armor of Unfeeling, Signet of Creation | "**your** spirits", "summoned creatures **you** control". Ownership is not in §8.4's filter list. |
| `InRangeOf(entity)` | every binding ritual | "within **its** range" — the spirit's own aura radius, not a fixed band. |
| `Hostile` / `Allied` filters | Spiritual Pain | "hostile summoned creatures", which is `IsSummoned` plus an allegiance filter. |

`Secondary(factor)` is already in §8.4 and is needed constantly: **seven** M1 skills apply 75%
of their damage to nearby foes. That construct is carrying more weight than its one-line
mention suggests.

### Actions

| Construct | Needed by | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SetStat(stat, value)` | Master of Magic | Sets attributes rather than adding. `ModifyStat` cannot express it. |
| `ReduceIncomingDamage { flat, percent, cap }` | Shielding Hands, Union, Armor of Unfeeling, Veil of Thorns, Armor of Sanctity, Shelter | Six skills, four different shapes: a flat reduction, a percentage, a percentage limited to a damage *source*, and a cap on a single hit as a share of maximum health. §8.4's `ModifyStat(damage multiplier)` covers only one. |
| `RechargeSkill(which)` | Pious Renewal | Recharges itself when it ends. |
| `ModifySkillCost(scope, amount)` | Soul Twisting | Scoped by skill type. Part of why it stays a handler. |
| `HoldBundle` / `DropBundle` | Protective Was Kaolai | Item spells are a whole mechanic: hold an item, gain something, drop it for an effect. |
| `SetUnblockable` | Crossfire, Magehunter Strike, Precision Shot, Wild Throw | Sometimes unconditional, sometimes gated on a filter, so it is an action rather than a flag. |
| `ModifyAdrenalineRate` | Infuriating Heat | "gain adrenaline twice as fast". |
| `ModifyProjectileSpeed` | Mighty Throw | |
| `ModifyActivation(target)` | Enchanter's Conundrum | Slows the *target's* casting, and only for enchantments — so it needs a skill-type scope. |
| `ImmuneToCriticals` | Armor of Unfeeling | |
| `FailSkill` | Mistrust | The triggered spell fails rather than being interrupted. Failing and interrupting differ in whether the cost is lost and whether recharge starts (§10.5 ENG-14), so they cannot share an action. |

### Events

§8.4's list covers most of what M1 needs. Missing:

| Event | Needed by |
| --- | --- |
| `OnSkillUsed` (by another creature) | Panic |
| `OnExperienceKill` | Air of Superiority |
| `OnBundleDropped` | Protective Was Kaolai |
| `OnConditionRemoved` | Cautery Signet |
| `OnEnchantmentRemoved` | Eremite's Attack, Drain Enchantment, Shatter Enchantment, Strip Enchantment |
| `OnSpiritDeath` | Life |

`OnDamageTaken`, `OnHit`, `OnBlocked`, `OnDeath`, `OnSpellCast`, `OnCreatureCreated` and
`OnEffectEnded` are all in §8.4 already and all get used.

### Stats a `ModifyStat` must reach

Collected from every skill that modifies one: armor (core and bonus), movement speed, attack
speed, activation time, recharge, adrenaline rate, projectile speed, block chance, health
regeneration, energy regeneration, maximum energy, damage taken (flat and percent), healing
received, **attribute rank**, and critical immunity.

**Attribute rank is the surprise.** Masochism grants "+2 to Death Magic and Soul Reaping",
which means a temporary rank change has to ripple through every scaled value on the bar. §10.14
anticipates this with `recompute(attr)`; this is the M1 skill that proves it is needed.

## 4. Effect families in M1

Hex · enchantment (including flash) · weapon spell · item spell · stance · shout · signet ·
binding ritual · nature ritual · skill (untyped) · attack skills (axe, bow, spear, scythe,
melee) · spell.

**Two one-at-a-time families appear**: stances (Sprint, Whirling Defense) and item spells
(Protective Was Kaolai — one bundle). Splinter Weapon is one weapon spell *per target*, which
is a third stacking shape.

## 5. Delayed effects are a shape, not an exception

Two M1 skills do nothing for a fixed time and then act:

- **Ancestors' Rage** — one second, then AoE damage.
- **Pious Renewal** — eight seconds, then energy, health and a self-recharge.

Both are an effect with an empty `while_active` and a populated `on_end`, which §8.4 already
supports. Worth naming because the natural reading of either description is "a delayed action",
and inventing a `Delay` action when `on_end` already exists would give two ways to say one
thing.

## 6. Where the design's own guesses were right

- **`Secondary(factor)` was correctly anticipated.** Seven skills need it.
- **`PerUnit` was correctly anticipated**, and only its quantity list was short.
- **The `Triggered` construct is the single most load-bearing thing in §8.4.** It is what
  turns twelve of the sixteen predicted handlers into ordinary data.

## 7. Open questions

1. **Is the 6% handler rate representative?** M1 is Mesmer-heavy. Re-measure at the first P7
   profession batch before revising §22's volume estimate.
2. **Master of Magic may be encodable** once `SetStat` exists. It is classed as a handler
   because of its energy-return interaction with attunements, which nothing else in M1 shares.
   Worth a second look in WP4.1.
3. **`ReduceIncomingDamage` has four shapes** and may want to be four constructs rather than
   one with four optional fields. T1.5.2 should decide with all six skills in front of it.
4. **Spirit auras need a range that is not a `RangeBand`** — the spirit's own, which varies by
   ritual type. `InRangeOf(entity)` is proposed, but where that range is stored is T1.5.3's
   problem.
