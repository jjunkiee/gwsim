# T0.5.2 Data licence details

**Task:** T0.5.2 (P0, WP0.5) · **Type:** Research · **Date:** 2026-09-22

***This is not legal advice.*** It records what the licence stewards and the wiki publish,
so that `data/LICENSE` and `data/ATTRIBUTION.md` can be written accurately.

> **Outcome, added 2026-09-22.** This research was done while `data/` was to be CC BY-SA
> 4.0 under Q38. On reviewing it, the owner chose **a single licence for the whole project,
> GPL-3.0-or-later** (**D33**), and `data/LICENSE` was deleted.
>
> The findings below stand as researched and are what justified the change:
> §2's one-way BY-SA → GPLv3 compatibility is the direction D33 travels in, and §4's
> finding that the wiki is **GFDL** is unaffected by the switch — GFDL is incompatible with
> GPL-3.0-or-later just as it was with CC BY-SA, so the facts-only rule in §4.2 is still
> load-bearing. Read §1 and §3 as historical: the canonical CC text and the BY-SA
> attribution form are no longer used.

## 1. Canonical CC BY-SA 4.0 legal code

- **Plain text:** <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode.txt>
  (`Content-Type: text/plain; charset=utf-8`, 20,138 bytes, 428 lines)
- **Human-readable summary (the "deed"):** <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>

`legalcode.en.txt` does **not** exist (404); the unsuffixed `.txt` is the English text.
`data/LICENSE` is a byte-for-byte copy of that file, downloaded on 2026-09-22 (T0.5.3).

## 2. Compatibility with GPLv3

Creative Commons lists exactly two non-CC licences as compatible with BY-SA 4.0 for the
ShareAlike clause:

| Licence | Declared compatible |
| --- | --- |
| Free Art License 1.3 | 2014-10-21 |
| **GPLv3** | 2015-10-08 |

**The compatibility is one-way.** In CC's own framing: contributions to adaptations of
BY-SA 4.0 material may be licensed under GPLv3, but contributions to adaptations of GPLv3
projects may **not** be licensed under BY-SA 4.0.

**What this means for gwsim.** The direction we rely on is the permitted one: `data/` is
BY-SA 4.0, and the GPL-3.0 code in `crates/` reads it. Keeping the two licences in separate
trees, each with its own `LICENSE`, is what makes this clean. We must not go the other way,
i.e. never relicense GPL-3.0 code or code-derived material into `data/` as BY-SA.

Source: <https://creativecommons.org/share-your-work/licensing-considerations/compatible-licenses/>

## 3. Recommended attribution form

Creative Commons recommends crediting title, author, source and licence, each linked where
a link exists. The line `ATTRIBUTION.md` gives reusers of gwsim data (T0.5.4):

> gwsim game data, by the gwsim contributors, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

## 4. The wiki's own copyright terms

From `GWW:Copyrights` (terms of use) and `GWW:Copyrighted content` (an official policy).
Summarised in our own words; nothing below is copied prose except the one short policy
sentence quoted in §4.3, which is quoted because its exact wording is the rule we follow.

### 4.1 Which licence covers editor text

**The GNU Free Documentation License — not CC BY-SA.** Contributors license their own
"Original Content" under the GFDL on editing.

Two caveats worth recording:

- The page states the version inconsistently: the contributor agreement says **GFDL 1.2 or
  any later version**, while the summary sentence just below says **GFDL 1.3 or later**.
  Either way it is GFDL, which is what matters to us.
- **The Feedback namespace is excluded.** Contributions posted in or to `Feedback:` are
  assigned to ArenaNet outright and are *not* GFDL. The extractor must therefore never
  treat `Feedback:` pages as reusable, even though the earlier research pass cites
  `Feedback:Game_updates/*` for balance history. Facts from update notes are still usable
  under §4.3, but their text is not.

### 4.2 This is the key licensing fact for gwsim

**GFDL is not compatible with CC BY-SA 4.0**, and the one-way GPLv3 compatibility in §2
does not help here either. So wiki prose can never be copied into `data/` and relicensed
as BY-SA. Our "facts only, expressed originally" rule is not a stylistic preference — it is
the only lawful route, and §8.10's ban on copied wiki prose is load-bearing.

### 4.3 The wiki's statement on facts

The policy page says, of taking material from sources whose licence is incompatible with
the wiki's own:

> "Factual information from incompatible sources may be used only if it is expressed
> originally. This does not apply to images."

This is precisely the mechanism gwsim applies one step further down the chain: we take
**facts** from the GFDL wiki and express them originally as RON data and generated
descriptions. The exclusion of images is equally explicit, and matches our absolute ban on
icons and art.

### 4.4 ArenaNet content and trademarks (C2)

- **ArenaNet Content** — anything from the game, the official site, manuals, guides,
  concept art, press and fansite kits — is **not** GFDL. All right, title and interest
  remain with ArenaNet and/or NCSOFT. The wiki may host it; we may not take it.
- **Trademarks:** NCSOFT, the interlocking NC logo, the ArenaNet logo, ArenaNet, Guild
  Wars and the campaign names are trademarks or registered trademarks of **NCSOFT
  Corporation**. The wiki is neither endorsed by nor affiliated with the rights holders,
  and states it cannot grant rights to use protected material.
- **No developer review:** the wiki states its articles are not consistently reviewed by
  ArenaNet or NCSOFT staff and carry no implied warranty of accuracy. This is independent
  support for the design's review statuses (`NumbersOnly` → `Draft` → `Reviewed`) and for
  treating wiki numbers as claims to be validated, not gospel.

### 4.5 Consequences carried into the repository

1. `data/ATTRIBUTION.md` credits the Guild Wars Wiki as the **source of the facts**, and
   states plainly that no wiki text, ArenaNet text or art is included (T0.5.4).
2. The `research/` corpus, which does contain wiki-derived prose, stays local and untracked
   (Q42, enforced by `/research/` in `.gitignore`, T0.4.2). This is now a licensing
   requirement, not just a tidiness one.
3. `CONTRIBUTING.md` (T0.6.1) must state that the wiki is GFDL, so contributors understand
   *why* prose may not be pasted in.
4. The extractor must skip the `Feedback:` namespace for text.

## 5. Note for P2: VPN exit IPs are refused

While researching this task, every request to `wiki.guildwars.com` returned **HTTP 403
Forbidden**, including plain article pages, with both an ordinary fetcher and the honest
EXT-4 User-Agent. Per EXT-7 the requests were stopped rather than worked around.

**Cause: the machine was on a VPN.** With the VPN off, the same URLs returned 200
immediately. The block is at the exit IP, not a bot policy, and nothing about our
User-Agent or request pattern was at fault.

Recorded because it will otherwise look like a crawl failure: **the extractor (P2) must run
with the VPN off**, and T2.1.1 should treat a blanket 403 as "check the network path"
before concluding the wiki has blocked the crawler.
