# T4.5.1 — Hero behaviour and the 2026 notes

**Task:** T4.5.1 (P4, WP4.5). **Status:** done. The rules are implemented in `crates/gwsim-engine/src/ai/hero.rs` and the shared evaluator `ai/skills.rs`, and tested in `crates/gwsim-engine/tests/hero_ai.rs`.

## Sources

- `research/Player_versus_Environment/Hero.md`, and the wiki's Hero behavior page (cached).
- The **Hero Usage** section of each M1 party skill's wiki page (cached in P2). These are paraphrased below and not quoted.
- The 2026 update notes: May 12, June 24, July 29 and August 26 (`Feedback:Game updates/2026…`).
- Where the notes and the Hero behavior page disagree, the notes win (A-026).

## General rules (AI-H1 to AI-H9)

| Rule | What gwsim does |
| --- | --- |
| **AI-H1 targeting** | The hero's locked target comes first. Next are the called target, then the target the player is attacking, then its own previous target. Failing all of those, in combat, it takes the foe with the lowest armor, then the lowest health. |
| **AI-H2 modes** | **Fight** goes after targets. **Guard** holds its formation point and fights only what comes within reach. **Avoid Combat** never attacks and uses only non-offensive skills. |
| **AI-H3 no coordination** | Each hero reads the party alone, so two heroes may cleanse the same hex or heal the same ally. |
| **AI-H4 instant interrupts** | Heroes react to a foe's cast inside their reaction delay (A-011 = 250 ms; interrupts 0 ms). This is `interrupt_now`. |
| **AI-H5 no double effects** | A hero never reapplies an effect that is already on its target. |
| **AI-H6 skill types** | Batteries go only on allies at or below half energy (Hero behavior, Blood is Power). Shouts wait for combat. Speed-boost shouts wait until the party moves. Passive spirits are cast in combat. |
| **AI-H7 2026 changes** | See the next section. |
| **AI-H8 no pre-casting** | Out of combat a hero casts only upkeep and maintained effects. Pre-fight casting comes from the tactics plan (WP4.7). |
| **AI-H9 disabled skills** | A skill the tactics plan disables is never used on the hero's own initiative. |

**One scoring function for every skill.** Instead of per-skill hints, the evaluator reads each skill's `SkillProfile` from its DSL encoding, so a newly encoded skill is used sensibly with no extra work (F4.21). The scores, highest first:

| Concern | Score |
| --- | --- |
| Raise the dead | 100 |
| Cleanse a hex | 60 |
| Interrupt | 55 |
| Heal | 50 + missing health |
| Battery, enchantment removal | 45 |
| Spirit | 40 |
| Summon | 38 |

| Bundle | 33 |
| Hex, party buff, creatures | 30 |
| Damage | 20 |

## What the 2026 notes change

- **June 24, melee stickiness.** Melee heroes stick to their previous target rather than moving between targets. Implemented as "previous target" in the AI-H1 chain.
- **June 24, AoE tolerance.** Heroes stay in an area effect until their health falls below **65%** (was 80%) before escaping. This is recorded but **not implemented**: M1 has no damage-over-time area to escape (F4.14).
- **June 24, melee auto-attacks.** Melee heroes put auto-attacks lower until their energy is low. No M1 hero is melee, so this is recorded only.
- **June 24, freezing and body-blocking.** Heroes freeze less and attack whatever body-blocks them. gwsim has no path-finding freeze to begin with.
- **May 12, July 29 and August 26.** These change Preservation, wells and Splinter Weapon. None of them is on an M1 bar since the caster variants were chosen (O6).

The Hero behavior page and the notes do not contradict each other on any rule M1 uses.

## Open points for review

- Several documented per-skill conditions are **not modelled as use rules**: the spirit conditions on Spirit Light, Lamentation and Spirit Transfer, Signet of Lost Souls' 50%, and Signet of Spirits' recast rule. Some differ from the page: Spirit Siphon triggers at 70% energy, not 80%; Life is cast in combat only; Armor of Unfeeling waits for spirits. They are listed for the owner's review of the AI and for P7, which encodes more skills.
- **"Incoming!" and "Fall Back!"** are cast only while the hero moves. In M1 the party stands still once combat starts, so hero 4's elite is almost never used (see [T4.10.7](T4.10.7-m1-relative-checks.md)).
