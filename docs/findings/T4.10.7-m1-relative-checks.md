## Triage

**Model bugs found and fixed:**

1. **A swing cut short by a skill blocked every later auto-attack** (F4.29). Any unit that used an attack skill stopped auto-attacking for the rest of the fight: the Kournan Guard, Zealot, Phalanx and Bowman, and all of the baseline's martial heroes. RC2's baseline exposed it: its dervish swung once and never again. After the fix, damage taken in the patrol rose from about 2,040 to 3,400 per fight.
2. **The Blood is Power hero sacrificed itself to death** in 12 of 32 fights (F4.30). The new A-044 holds a sacrifice back until the user would keep 25% health. This lifted the patrol win rate from about 95% to 98%.

**What remains, and why it is probably a property of the model rather than a bug:**

- **Blood is Power is a net cost.** With the floor in place, the variant without it still clears 5 s faster with fewer deaths. At the wiki's 33% sacrifice, hero 5 spends about 1,700 health a fight on it. Its targets gain energy, but the M1 party is limited by healing and deaths, not energy. Heroes 1–3 end fights with 5–10 energy either way, because their skills are recharge-bound. Raising the floor to 50% did not change the verdict. **Candidates for the owner:** the hero battery rule (spamming at 50% energy is documented and is what gwsim does), the value of energy to the Mesmer heroes, and whether foes should focus the low-health BiP hero as hard as they do.
- **"Incoming!" is never used.** gwsim's hero rule casts speed-boost shouts only while the hero moves, and the M1 party stands still in combat, so the elite's heal-while-moving never applies. In the game the party moves more: it approaches, re-positions and escapes area damage (not modelled, F4.14).
- **The Mesmer elites make no significant difference.** Panic and Energy Surge each remove one skill from one of three Mesmer heroes. The other two still interrupt and drain, and the freed energy goes to the rest of the bar. At 98% wins, 256 paired runs cannot resolve a 1–2 point difference.
- **Off-role swaps are often neutral.** In a model where the party is healing-limited, swapping a damage skill for a protection skill (Reversal of Fortune, Remove Hex) rarely hurts. RC1's 90% rule assumes every such swap is clearly worse, which does not hold when the benchmark already wins almost every fight.

## For the owner

RC1 is reported in CI without failing the build (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`). The options are:

1. **Accept a documented exception.** RC1 would then pass on the variants that change the model's bottleneck (spirits, attributes, gear), and hero 4's elite and random swaps would be excluded.
2. **Calibrate and re-run.** The places to look are foe damage (the Guard deals about 40% of all damage taken), the battery and sacrifice AI (A-044 and the Blood of the Master note in [T4.5.1](T4.5.1-hero-ai.md)), and movement in combat (area escape, speed boosts).
3. **Make RC1 harder to fail by chance**, for example by judging it on the M1 set rather than the patrol alone, where the harder fights (the chain) separate the variants better.
