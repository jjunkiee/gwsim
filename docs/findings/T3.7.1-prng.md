# T3.7.1 — Choosing the PRNG

**Date:** 2026-09-23 · **Status:** Decided · **Feeds:** T3.7.2 (`crates/gwsim-engine/src/rng.rs`)

## Decision

**`rand_chacha::ChaCha8Rng`, pinned to `rand_chacha = "=0.10.0"`**, with `rand_core 0.10`
for the `Rng` trait.

## Why

| Question | ChaCha8 (`rand_chacha`) | PCG64 MCG (`rand_pcg`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Output portable across platforms | yes: a specified stream cipher, endian-independent | yes |
| Output stable across crate versions | documented as a value-stability promise within a minor line | documented likewise |
| Independent streams | yes: 64-bit seed plus a stream id; seeding from a hash is also sound | seeding from a hash only |
| Speed | a few ns per `u64` with 8 rounds; far below the cost of a skill evaluation | slightly faster |
| Statistical quality | cryptographic-grade | excellent for simulation |

The engine derives thousands of streams per evaluation — one per (run seed, purpose,
unit) — and the purpose of each is to stay independent of every other when a build
changes. A cipher keyed by a hash of those three values gives that without having to
reason about correlated PCG increments. Speed is not the bottleneck: 64 M0 fights
take 0.18 s in an unoptimised build, almost none of it drawing numbers.

## How streams are derived

The state `run ⊕ purpose × C₁ ⊕ (unit + 1) × C₂` (with odd 64-bit constants, and no
unit term for shared streams) is stepped through SplitMix64 four times to fill a
32-byte ChaCha key, then `ChaCha8Rng::from_seed(key)`. Purposes are numbered
explicitly (Hits 1, Crits 2, SkillChance 3, Ai 4, Spawn 5, Consumables 6) so reordering
the enum cannot change a result. Units are numbered by stable slot and foe order.

## Why the pin is exact

A seed's result must not change when dependencies update (ENG-43). The `=` pin makes any
change of generator an explicit edit to `Cargo.toml`, and the determinism tests
(`crates/gwsim-engine/tests/determinism.rs`) and the combat-log snapshot catch a change
in output.
