- **What this is:** every M1 skill (§20.3) as the extractor parses it from the
  cached wiki page, **numbers only** — no description text (EXT-7).
- **How each row was checked:**
  - **69 rows, "✓ vs P1":** compared field by field with `.cache/m1_skills.json`,
    the independent record T1.3.1 and T1.5.1 made from the same pages on
    2026-09-22. Id, energy, adrenaline, sacrifice, upkeep, overcast, activation
    and recharge all agree for every skill (0 differences). Every scaled row
    whose endpoints the P1 record quotes agrees too.
  - **2 rows, "✓ vs spike page":** Lamentation and Blood of the Master joined
    the party after P1 read its pages (E6), so they were checked against the
    spike copies fetched in T2.1: costs, times and every progression row agree.

  as `x…y…z`, and were checked arithmetically instead: the 75% area rows of
  Cry of Frustration, Mistrust and Enchanter's Conundrum (the 2026 Mesmer
  rule), Energy Surge's target damage (7 × energy lost, flagged special
  rounding), Spirit Transfer's healing (5 × spirit health lost) and Spirit
  Siphon's energy gain.

  least 10 rows including every special-rounding row (Energy Surge is the only
