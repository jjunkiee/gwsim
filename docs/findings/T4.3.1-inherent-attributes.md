# T4.3.1 — Inherent attribute effects

**Task:** T4.3.1 (P4, WP4.3). **Status:** done. The effects are implemented in T4.3.2, where each one sits (listed below), and tested in `crates/gwsim-engine/tests/m1_mechanics.rs`.

## Sources

The Guild Wars Wiki pages for each attribute (Fast Casting, Soul Reaping, Spawning Power, Strength, Expertise, Divine Favor, Mysticism, Leadership, Death Magic, Critical Strikes), read through the extractor cache. Where the Reforged update changed a rule, the current page text is used (§20.4).

## The rules

| Attribute | Effect as implemented | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fast Casting** (Mesmer primary) | Spells and signets activate ×0.5^(rank/15) (signets: A-017). Mesmer spells recharge 3% faster per rank. Done in WP3.4 and rechecked. | `pipeline.rs` |
| **Soul Reaping** (Necromancer primary) | +1 energy per rank when a non-spirit creature dies within 2508 (the Soul Reaping page's range). At most 3 times per 15 seconds (the Reforged rule), and never above maximum energy. | `regen.rs` `soul_reaping` |
| **Spawning Power** (Ritualist primary) | Creatures the Ritualist makes get +4% health per rank (A-015 gives spirit health as 20 × level × (1 + 0.04 × SP)). Weapon spells last longer per rank, but no M1 bar carries a weapon spell, so this is not implemented yet. | `creatures.rs` |
| **Strength** (Warrior primary) | Attack skills get +1% armor penetration per rank. | `attack.rs` |
| **Expertise** (Ranger primary) | −4% energy cost per rank on attack skills, rituals, touch skills and Ranger skills, for a Ranger primary. | `pipeline.rs` `energy_cost` |
| **Divine Favor** (Monk primary) | A Monk spell that targets an ally also heals that target for 3.2 × rank, rounded. An untargeted Monk spell heals the caster instead. | `inherent.rs` |
| **Mysticism** (Dervish primary) | −4% energy cost per rank on Dervish enchantment spells. In PvE, +1 armor per rank while enchanted. | `pipeline.rs`, `inherent.rs` |
| **Leadership** (Paragon primary) | +2 energy for each ally a shout or chant reaches, capped at one ally per two ranks. | `inherent.rs` |
| **Death Magic** (minion cap) | A necromancer controls at most 2 + rank / 2 minions (rounded down). A new one past the cap makes the oldest crumble. | `creatures.rs` |
| **Critical Strikes** | Recorded for later; no M1 unit is an Assassin. | — |

## Notes

- **Primary only.** Every one of these is a primary-attribute effect. The engine checks the unit's primary profession, so a secondary Ranger gets no Expertise discount.
- **The Reforged Soul Reaping rule** (three times per 15 seconds) matters to the M1 minion master, hero 4. Without the limit, a large minion wave would refill its energy on every death.
- **Rounding.** Divine Favor rounds half away from zero, like other healing (T3.5.1). The Leadership cap rounds down.
