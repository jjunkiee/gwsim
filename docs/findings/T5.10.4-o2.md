# T5.10.4 — O2: Solo Resto

**Task:** T5.10.4 (P5, WP5.10). **Status:** run and recorded. The eight added skills are still `Draft` (fallout F5.17), so these results are provisional. Absolute numbers are uncalibrated (D22).

## Solo Resto against Dual Resto

`gwsim compare mesmerway-solo-resto m1-mesmerway --situations m1 --runs 256`. Both parties carry the M1 player, and runs are paired on seeds.

| Situation | Solo Resto | Dual Resto | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dummies HM | 33.5 s | 29.2 s | Dual better |
| Kournan melee heavy HM | 35.6 s | 40.1 s | Solo better |
| Kournan caster heavy HM | 20.8 s | 25.3 s | Solo better |
| Kournan healer heavy HM | 25.2 s, 0.00 deaths | 32.7 s, 0.06 deaths | Solo better |
| Kournan patrol HM | 36.0 s, 99.6%, 0.28 deaths | 46.2 s, 98.4%, 0.70 deaths | Solo better |
| Kournan patrol HM chain | 82.9 s, 97.7% | 100.0 s, 97.7% | Solo better |

In the model, **Solo Resto is the stronger team** in every real fight. It loses only on the dummies, where the Ineptitude Mesmer's anti-attack skills have nothing to work on. This agrees with RC1's picture of Dual Resto ([T4.10.7](T4.10.7-m1-relative-checks.md)). The Minion Master and its "Incoming!" add little in gwsim, and a second damage-and-control Mesmer adds a lot.

The best build keeps the Ritualist/Paragon pair and most of the bar: "Stand Your Ground!", "Find Their Weakness!", Ancestors' Rage, Splinter Weapon, Lamentation and "Fall Back!". It adds Signet of Spirits and "Never Surrender!", and drops "Incoming!" and Spirit Siphon. The code is `OAmjIykjJP14rZ92bix8xUudM`. The other frontier members put a binding ritual such as Union on the slot, trading a little clear time for fewer deaths.

## Caveats

- Splinter Weapon is for melee allies, and this team has none except minions. Its value in gwsim comes from the hero's own wand attacks. The optimiser keeps it, but its encoding is simplified (F5.14).
- The Stalwart insignias' physical armor is not modelled (F5.15), so the page's Splinter Support hero is slightly weaker in gwsim than in the game.
