# T3.10.1 — The 8 M0 skills and the M0 hand calculations

**Date:** 2026-09-23 · **Status:** Done · **Feeds:** T3.10.3 (encodings), T3.10.7
(`crates/gwsim-cli/tests/m0.rs`)

Every number below is worked by hand from the formulas cited, and is checked exactly
by an M0 acceptance test. The wiki pages were read in full from the local cache
(`.cache/wiki/`, crawled 2026-09-23); the behaviour is recorded here in our own words.

## Inputs

**Player** (the M0 party, `data/parties/m0-player.ron`): Mesmer, template
`OQBTAUBPQaJ4EY6x0BAAAAAAuE`, wand and focus, five Prodigy's insignias.

| Attribute | Points | Bonuses | Effective rank |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fast Casting | 10 | minor rune +1 | **11** |
| Domination Magic | 12 | superior rune +3, headgear +1 | **16** |
| Inspiration Magic | 8 | minor rune +1 | **9** |

Derived (T1.4.1): maximum energy **42**, **4** energy pips, maximum health **465**.

**Dummy** (`data/creatures/dummies.ron`): level 26, 40 energy that never regenerates,
armor 60 against everything, 480 health, fleshy, gives experience. Three stand in the
encounter: dummy 2 is 100 gwinches from dummy 1 (inside adjacent, 166); dummy 3 is 224
from dummy 1 (inside nearby, 252, but not adjacent).

## Formulas used

| Rule | Formula | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scaling | `round(at0 + rank × (at15 − at0) / 15)` | Skill |
| Fast Casting, activation | `× 0.5^(rank/15)`; rank 11 → **0.60151** | Fast Casting |
| Fast Casting reach | spells and signets; non-Mesmer skills under 2 s are unaffected | Fast Casting |
| PvE Fast Casting, recharge | Mesmer spells only: `× (1 − 0.03 × rank)`; rank 11 → **0.67** | Fast Casting |
| Recharge rounding | to the nearest second | Recharge time |
| Activation rounding (ours) | to the nearest millisecond, half up | modelling choice (time model B) |
| Aftercast | 0.75 s for spells and signets, and for Air of Superiority despite its 0 s activation | Aftercast delay |
| Mesmer "damage" | untyped damage ignores armor, so the dummy's armor and level do not matter | Armor-ignoring damage |
| "75% of that damage" | 75% of the rounded target figure, then rounded | the skill pages; see the note on Mistrust |

## Results

| Skill | Activation | Recharge | Cost | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Energy Surge | 2 × 0.60151 = **1,203 ms** | 15 × 0.67 = 10.05 → **10 s** | 5 | energy loss `round(1 + 16×9/15)` = **11**; target takes 7 × 11 = **77**; other nearby foes take 0.75 × 77 = 57.75 → **58** |
| Mistrust | **1,203 ms** | 12 × 0.67 = 8.04 → **8 s** | 10 | a 6 s hex; the next spell the foe casts **on one of your allies** fails; the base damage is `round(10 + 16×70/15)` = 85, and the foe **and** every other nearby foe take 0.75 × 85 → **64** (the 75% applies to the target too since 2026-06-24) |
| Unnatural Signet | 1 × 0.60151 = **602 ms** | **10 s** (a signet: no PvE reduction) | 0 | target takes `round(15 + 16×60/15)` = **79**; only if it is hexed or enchanted, foes adjacent to it take `round(5 + 16×45/15)` = **53** |
| Cry of Frustration | 0.25 × 0.60151 = **150 ms** | 20 × 0.67 = 13.4 → **13 s** | 10 | only if the target is using a skill: the target and foes in the area are interrupted; target takes **79**, others 0.75 × 79 → **59** |
| Spiritual Pain | **602 ms** | 7 × 0.67 = 4.69 → **5 s** | 5 | target takes **79**; hostile summoned creatures in the area take `round(25 + 16×100/15)` = **132** |

### Air of Superiority's benefits

Only a kill that gives the user experience counts (the page's note: at least 1 XP).
Each such kill grants one benefit, drawn with the page's chances:

| Benefit | Chance |
| --- | --- |
| all conditions removed | 30% |
| double experience (no effect in a simulation) | 20% |
| healed for 50 | 20% |
| gain 5 energy | 20% |
| all skills recharged | 10% |

Who "earns experience" from a kill is **A-036**: a party member alive and within 5,020
gwinches (about the radar) of the dying foe. The draw uses the user's skill-chance
stream.

## Notes and decisions

- **Energy Surge's damage follows the energy actually lost.** A foe with 4 energy loses
  4 and takes 28 (21 to others). Tested.
- **Mistrust's target takes 75% too.** The description reads as full damage to the target
  and 75% to others, but the June 24, 2026 update notes record that the area reduction
  "also applies to target foe", so Mistrust never deals its full figure. This was missed
  when M0 was first written (the tests said 85) and corrected in P4 with a `Reduced`
  selector: every foe it reaches takes **64**. The page's progression row (8…50…60) is
  the 75% pre-rounded per rank and gives 63 at rank 16; the encoding applies the rule to
  the rounded 85.
- **Mistrust is data, not a handler.** The plan listed it as a handler (T3.10.4), but the
  DSL's `Triggered` construct expresses it fully (as T1.5.1 predicted), with a filter
  for "cast on one of your allies". Logged as an owner decision in the fallout list.
- **Fast Casting at 11 is 0.60151, not 0.6013.** An earlier draft of this calculation
  used 0.6013 and so gave 601 ms for the 1 s skills. The correct figure rounds to
  **602 ms**.
- **Unnatural Signet is a Mesmer signet**, so Fast Casting shortens it (A-017).
- **Power Drain's "or chant"** has no chant to test against in M0; the encoding checks
  `CastingSpell` only. Chants arrive with the Kournan foes if any carry one (WP4.3).
