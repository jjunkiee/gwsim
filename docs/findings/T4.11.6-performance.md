# T4.11.6 — Performance: hot paths, optimisations and the recorded target

**Tasks:** T4.11.3 (hot paths), T4.11.4 (optimise), T4.11.6 (verify and record). **Status:** done. The patrol median is **4.7 ms**, under the 10 ms target (D19).

## Machine and method

- AMD Ryzen 7 7800X3D (8 cores, 16 threads), 64 GB, Windows 11 Pro. Rust 1.98, `--release` with fat LTO and `codegen-units = 1`.
- `cargo bench -p gwsim-engine --bench fights`: criterion, 10 samples. Each iteration is one fight on the next seed of a 16-seed list, with logging off, on one core.
- Measured at the commit after d52d80f on branch `m1/p4` (the WP4.10 commit).

## Results

| Benchmark | Median per fight |
| --- | --- |
| Kournan patrol, HM (8 party, 8 foes, plus spirits and minions) | **4.73 ms** |
| Two-fight chain (patrol, rest, patrol) | 11.0 ms (5.5 ms per fight) |
| Healer-heavy group, HM | 3.89 ms |
| Synthetic 8-v-16 stress fight (every patrol foe doubled) | 5.27 ms |

The stress fight is quick because the party is overrun early: fewer seconds are simulated, not less work per second.

## Hot paths found (T4.11.3)

They were found with temporary counters and timing, and are listed here in the order the fixes paid off. The shares were not kept when the instrumentation was removed.

1. **Effect scans for stat queries.** Every stat query walked every effect definition on the unit, and on every spirit whose aura reached it, whatever the stat.
2. **AI decisions every tick.** Each controller re-scored its whole bar every 50 ms.
3. **Trigger dispatch.** Every event visited every effect to look for a matching trigger.
4. **Gear effects on every stat query.**
5. The rest was spread thin: movement, regeneration and the event queue.

## Optimisations (T4.11.4)

- **Per-definition stat bitmasks.** Each effect definition records which stats its `while_active` can change (`FightSkill.def_stats`), plus flags for reductions, critical immunity and triggers (`def_flags`). A query skips any definition whose mask misses the stat. Gear effects carry the same mask.
- **Aura and caster lists.** `aura_spirits` lists spirits with an aura, and `caster_effects` lists effects that give their caster a modifier, so neither needs a scan of all units.
- **An `any_upkeep` flag**, which skips the upkeep pass when nothing is maintained.
- **Decision cadence.** A controller decides at its reaction delay (A-010, A-011, A-012), not every tick. Hero interrupts still react within the tick (`interrupt_now`).
- **Release profile:** fat LTO and `codegen-units = 1`.

Taken together, the patrol went from 47 ms to 7.5 ms per fight. Tactics then shortened fights, and the fixes of WP4.10 changed the fight, which brought it to the 4.7 ms above. The determinism tests were re-run after each change, and every seed's digest was unchanged except where behaviour was meant to change.
