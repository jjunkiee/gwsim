The per-type defaults live in `gwsim_data::core::SkillType` (`default_aftercast_ms`,
`uses_action_queue`, `usable_while_knocked_down`); they matched T1.1.3 and needed no
change. The exceptions the Aftercast delay page lists by name (Dwarven Stability,
Verata's Gaze and so on) are not in M1 and are not yet modelled; see the fallout list.

## Open points answered

**What does Fast Casting affect in PvE?** Spells and signets. A non-Mesmer spell or
signet is unaffected unless its base activation is **2 s or more**. So every Mesmer
signet is shortened (A-017 resolved: yes). Rank 11 gives ×0.60151.

**How does PvE Fast Casting cut recharge?** By 3% per rank on **every Mesmer spell**
(not signets): `× (1 − 0.03 × rank)`. It multiplies with item and effect recharge
modifiers, which are clamped to −50% first. The total is then rounded to the nearest
second.

**Overcast.** Paid at the start of activation, so it is lost to an interrupt or cancel.
It lowers maximum energy by 3, 5 or 10, stacking, and lowers current energy only if
current exceeds the new maximum. It recovers at 1 point of maximum energy per 3 seconds.
No M1 player skill has it; the engine carries `overcast` on the unit for P7.

**Fail vs fizzle.** A skill **fails** when a requirement is not met or an effect forces it
(Mistrust): the cost is lost and it **recharges instantly**; nothing prevents a failure.
A **fizzle** is any activation that starts and does not finish: an **interrupt** (cost
lost, **full recharge**), a cancel (no recharge), an invalid target (no recharge) or a
disable. gwsim models interrupt, fail and cancel; a target that dies during activation
makes the skill fail (A-040).

**Weapon half-cast and half-recharge mods.** Each is rolled once per use, for spells only,
from the user's skill-chance stream. How two items' chances combine is undocumented:
gwsim takes the largest (A-038).

**Hard-mode foe recharge (A-032).** Hard mode says foes have "reduced recharge time" but
gives no amount. A-032 stays **Pending**: the engine applies **no** reduction and marks
every hard-mode foe recharge as touching A-032, so any result that depends on it says so.
Hard-mode foes do have **halved activation for skills longer than 2 s** (stated, and
implemented).

## Other rules the pipeline follows

- **Costs** (energy, adrenaline) are paid at the start of activation; **sacrifice** is paid
  only on success (ENG-13).
- **Recharge** starts when the skill completes, or at the interrupt.
- **Knockdown** stops an activation but is not an interrupt, so interrupt-prevention
  does not help against it. The default knockdown lasts 2 s.
- **Dazed** doubles spell activation, interrupts a spell when applied, and lets any hit
  interrupt a spell.
- An **attack skill's** effects land when its attack hits (mid-swing, A-035).
