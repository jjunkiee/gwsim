## RC5 (T5.9.4)

**Pass:** it is better in all six situations, and never worse. That build (Arcane Echo, Energy Surge, Cry of Frustration, Unnatural Signet, Power Spike, Spiritual Pain, Mistrust, Air of Superiority) stays close to the PvX bar. It swaps Power Drain for Power Spike, which suggests the PvX bar is near a local optimum of the model.

## Triage (T5.9.5)

The player then takes enough attacks that a 50%+ block stance repays a slot, and the saved healing turns into faster clears.
