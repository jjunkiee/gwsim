**Equipment given on the page:** for the Splinter Support hero, a superior Vigor rune, three Attunement runes, five Stalwart insignias and a 40/40 Channeling set. The page shows no gear for the Ineptitude Mesmer. It mirrors the Domination heroes, with superior Illusion in place of superior Domination (fallout F5.16).

The page offers the Minion Master from Dual Resto as the alternative for the Variable Hero. Solo Resto is thus Dual Resto with the Minion Master replaced by the Ineptitude Mesmer, the Signet of Spirits resto replaced by the Splinter Support Ritualist, and Life on the BiP hero.
