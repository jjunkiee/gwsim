**All eight agree with §20.2.** Two things §20.2 left unsaid are now settled:

1. **The "a / b" armor pairs are physical against elemental.** The Guard's
   116 is blunt, piercing and slashing; its 96 is cold, earth, fire and
   lightning. The Bowman is the other way round: 70 physical, 100 elemental.
   Every table states **level 20**. The seeded files record the elemental
   figure as the default and list the physical types that differ, with
   `level_context: Some(20)`.
2. **Level variants.** The Phalanx and Scribe pages list a level-16 loadout
   and a "level(s) 20 and above" loadout. The parser treats such headings as
   levels of one foe rather than alternative loadouts, and the seeded bar is
   the one covering level 20. The Guard's hammer and sword forms and the
   Bowman's Jahai Bluffs form are genuine loadout variants and are kept as
   variants (A-007).

## What the wiki does not give

- **Zealot and Phalanx have no attribute ranks at all.** A-005 fills only
  hard-mode ranks *from* normal-mode ones, so for these two there is nothing
  to fill from. WP4.2 must supply normal-mode ranks too (fallout F2.7).
- **Hard-mode ranks** are given only for the Bowman, Seer and Oppressor. The
  other five fall back to A-005, which the seeded files cite.
- **No page gives a weapon**, so every seeded foe cites A-004.
- **Variant skills outside M1.** The Guard's hammer and sword forms and the
  Bowman's Jahai Bluffs form name 12 skills no M1 bar uses (Mighty Blow,
  Staggering Blow, "Shields Up!", "Watch Yourself!", Healing Signet, Seeking
  Blade, Forked Arrow, Keen Arrow, Pin Down, Prepared Shot, Read the Wind,
  Resurrection Signet). They were seeded `NumbersOnly` so the foe files'
  references resolve; nothing simulates them.
