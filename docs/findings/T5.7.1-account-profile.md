## Upgrades

The wiki's unlocking of runes and insignias is account-wide. Collecting an item unlocks it for PvP characters, and PvE characters simply own the items. The profile therefore records `available_upgrades` as "everything" or a set of slugs, and does not model how they were obtained.

## Melandru's Accord

It limits each character to the skills that character has learned, and heroes to their starting, trainer-bought and hero-trainer skills. It switches off account titles and their passive effects, and forbids mercenary heroes. In gwsim:

- a profile may list `learned_skills` per character; under the Accord, a human slot's pool is limited to its character's list;
- title ranks are all 0 in any situation with the Accord on;
- the heroes' allowed sources are **not** modelled. Every skill of the hero's professions stays available, because the data does not record hero starting skills (fallout F5.9).

## The profile

`AccountProfile { name, unlocked_skills: All | Only(set), title_ranks, heroes_owned: All | Only(set), eotn_owned, available_upgrades: All | Only(set), learned_skills }`.

- The default has everything unlocked and every title at its maximum (Q14).
- Profiles are RON files in the user directory's `profiles/` folder.
- `gwsim profile list | show | new | path | set <name> title <track> <rank> | unlock | lock <name> skill <slug…> | hero <name> add|remove <hero>` manages them.
