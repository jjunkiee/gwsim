## Speed modifiers

- Speed boosts and snares multiply. The combined effect is capped at **+34%** and
  **−50%** (Speed boost); a handful of named skills exceed the cap.
- **Flail plus "Fall Back!"** (§17.2): 0.67 × 1.33 = **0.891**, 89.1% of base speed.
  Tested in `crates/gwsim-engine/tests/wiki_examples.rs`.
- Hard-mode foes move **about 33% faster** (Hard mode).

## Body blocking

- In PvE, **friendly characters walk through each other**; foes block the party and the
  party blocks foes (Body block). gwsim therefore only blocks between opposing teams.
- There is no vertical axis.

## Projectiles: leading and missing

- A projectile **leads its target** from the target's position and velocity when fired,
  so a target that changes speed or direction afterwards can dodge it. Faster projectiles
  are harder to dodge (Projectile).
- The page gives no tolerance. gwsim computes the intercept point at launch, and a
  projectile hits if the target is within **two collision radii** of that point when it
  lands (our choice, recorded against A-002). A stationary or steadily moving target is
  always hit; one that turns or stops early can be missed.
- Projectile spells are not stopped by "block projectile" effects (Projectile).

## Engine decisions recorded here

- Positions are `f32` gwinches on a plane; ranges are compared on squared distances, so
  no square root decides a range check.
- A unit ordered to use a skill out of range walks until it is inside the skill's range,
  then casts (ENG-11). Tested in `tests/scenarios.rs`.
