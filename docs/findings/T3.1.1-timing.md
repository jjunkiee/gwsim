## Per-type timing rules

| Skill type | Activation | Aftercast | Queued (can't start mid-action) | Usable while knocked down | Usable during another activation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spell (not flash) | stated | ¾ s | yes | no | no |
| Flash enchantment | none | none | yes | no | **no** (explicitly) |
| Signet | stated | ¾ s (not *Dolyak Signet*) | yes | no | no |
| Chant, echo, glyph, ritual, form | stated | ¾ s | yes | no | no |
| Shout, stance, pet attack | none | none | **no** | **yes** | **yes** |
| Attack skill, no stated activation | first half of the next attack interval | none (the other half is the attack's return) | yes | no | no |
| Attack skill, stated activation | stated, scaled by attack speed; the hit lands half-way | none | yes | no | no |
| Untyped "Skill" | stated | ¾ s | yes | no | no |
| *Air of Superiority* (M0) | none | **¾ s** (anomaly) | yes | no | no |

- **Default aftercast is ¾ s** for anything with an activation time, and
  nothing reduces it (Fast Casting included).
- **The queue.** A queued skill waits for the current action; an interrupt
  clears the queue. Shouts, stances and pet attacks bypass it.
- **Retargeting or moving** during activation cancels the skill (cost paid,
  no recharge, no aftercast).

## Attacks

- The attack interval is the weapon's (axe, sword, dagger 1.33 s; scythe and
  spear 1.5 s; hammer, staff and wand 1.75 s; bows 2.025–2.7 s). IAS and DAS
  change the length of each attack, stack multiplicatively, and are capped at
  +33% and −50%.
- **Where in the interval the hit lands is not documented** for auto-attacks.
  The engine lands it half-way, matching the documented rule for attack
  skills without an activation time. Recorded as **A-035**.

## Regeneration

- Health: 2 HP/s per pip; energy: 1 energy per 3 s per pip. Net capped at
  ±10 pips, the cap applied after summing.
- The wiki does not say whether the game applies regeneration continuously or
  in ticks. The engine integrates it on its 50 ms tick in fixed point
  (T3.1.4), which is exact for whole-second totals.

## AI reaction and decision timing

As expected, the wiki says nothing numeric: A-010 (foes), A-011 (heroes: 0 for
interrupts, "never late") and A-012 (the human plan, 250 ms) remain the only
sources. The engine lets a controller decide on every 50 ms tick once its
reaction delay has passed after the unit's last action ended.
