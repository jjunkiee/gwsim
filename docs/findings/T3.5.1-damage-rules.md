# T3.5.1 — Damage and healing rules, with worked examples

**Date:** 2026-09-23 · **Status:** Done · **Feeds:** T3.5.2–T3.5.9 (`combat.rs`, `damage.rs`, `regen.rs`)

Sources: `research/Game_mechanics/` (Damage calculation, Damage type, Critical hit, Heal,
Health regeneration, Armor penetration, Block, Life stealing) and the cached pages
Damage calculation, Armor calculation and Critical hit, read 2026-09-23. Every worked
example below is a test in `crates/gwsim-engine/tests/wiki_examples.rs`.

## Strike level and the damage formula

- **Weapons:** strike level is `5 × rank` up to `(level + 4) / 2`, then `+2` per rank beyond.
  At level 20 the knee is rank 12 (strike level 60).
- **Skills, and wands and staves without mastery:** strike level `3 × level`.
- **Damage:** `base × 2^((strike level − armor level) / 40)`.

| Worked example | Expected | gwsim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A level 15 caster's skill damage vs the level 20 reference | 77.1% | 0.7711 |
| Level 30 caster, 70 base damage vs armor 60 | ≈ 117.7 | 117.72 |
| Weapon rank 8 vs rank 12, level 20 | 70.7% | 0.7071 |
| Weapon rank 16 vs rank 12, level 20 | 114.9% | 1.1487 |

## Armor calculation

1. Start from the struck piece's armor against the damage type (core).
2. Add bonuses: if the **positive** bonuses total **26 or more**, add `max(25, largest)`
   and ignore reductions (the documented bug, **A-031**); otherwise add the net, but a net
   reduction cannot take armor below `min(core, 60)`.
3. Apply armor penetration: `floor(armor × (1 − penetration))` (**A-023**: the wiki's
   own example lands ±1 from any single rounding rule).
4. Add special armor (outside the cap).

| Worked example | Expected | gwsim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core 106, net bonus −25 | 81 | 81 |
| … with 25% penetration | 61 (±1, A-023) | 60 |
| … plus 24 special | 85 (±1) | 84 |

## Which damage respects armor (§10.7)

| Type | Armor |
| --- | --- |
| Blunt, piercing, slashing; cold, earth, fire, lightning; chaos; dark | respects armor |
| Holy | **ignores** armor from skills; respects it from weapons |
| Shadow | always ignores armor |
| Typeless (a skill naming no type) | ignores armor |

Mesmer wands and staves deal **chaos** damage, which respects armor (A-037 covers the
caster-weapon type by profession). Found during this task: the interpreter treated only
typeless skill damage as armor-ignoring; holy and shadow skill damage now ignore armor
too.

## Critical hits

- The base chance depends on the attacker's and defender's levels and the weapon
  mastery rank; gwsim uses the community formula
  `0.05 × 2^((8·Lₐ + 4·r + 6·min(r, (Lₐ+4)/2) − 15·L_d − 100)/40) × (1 − r/100) + r/100`.
  The wiki gives no worked figure, only the rule that a level 20 attacker's base rate
  always exceeds its mastery rank as a percentage, which is tested for ranks 0–16
  against defenders of level 1–30.
- A critical hit deals the weapon's **maximum** damage and adds 20 to the strike level,
  a factor of √2 ≈ 1.414 (tested).
- Critical Strikes and skill bonuses add to the chance; a moving target struck from behind
  in melee is always critical. Neither appears in M1's player bar; both are P7 work.

## Order of modifiers (A-022)

Armor first, then the dealer's and the target's damage multipliers (uncapped), then the
target's reductions in the order its effects were applied, each crediting its skill with
the damage it prevented (§14.2). No damage cap per packet or per second applies in M1.

## Healing

- Healing modifiers multiply. The total **reduction** is capped at **−40%**; increases are
  uncapped. Deep Wound is one of the reductions (−20%) and also lowers maximum health.
- "Health gain" is not healing and ignores healing modifiers.
- Healing cannot exceed maximum health; the excess is recorded as overhealing.

## Regeneration and "in combat"

- One pip is 2 health per second (+3 for 10 s is 60 health). Pips sum and are clamped to
  ±10.
- **Natural regeneration:** after 5 s out of combat, +1 pip, then +1 every 2 s, up to +7.
- **In combat** means losing health, attacking, using spells or signets on foes, or being
  targeted by a foe's spell or signet. Self-healing, shouts, and being attacked without
  losing health do not count.
- Energy pips: one pip is 1/3 energy per second.

## Adrenaline

One strike is 25 units. A hit grants one strike to the attacker's adrenaline skills; the
target gains adrenaline from damage taken (1 unit per 1% of maximum health lost).
