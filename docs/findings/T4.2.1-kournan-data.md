## Rules decided here

- **Omitted ranks (A-042).** Some attributes a bar uses are not listed on the foe's page, and two pages list no ranks at all. Such an attribute takes the highest rank the page lists. With nothing listed, it is 14 for a martial profession and 15 for a caster, matching what the other Kournan pages show. Hard mode then applies A-005 as for any foe.
- **Ranks and armor in hard mode.** Hard mode raises the level to 26, and levels scale health and armor through the core tables (`derived::foe_armor_with`). The page's armor figures are at level 20 (`level_context`).
- **Weapons (A-004).** Foe pages do not give weapon damage. Each foe uses the player weapon of its type at maximum requirement. Casters hold a staff and archers a flatbow.
- **Variants.** The Guard has axe, sword and hammer loadouts. The patrol uses the axe (A-007). The hammer variant now carries its own armor through `FoeVariant.armor` (F4.22). The variant-only skills stay `NumbersOnly` (F4.35).
- **Ranks (A-042 in the foe files).** The Guard, Zealot, Phalanx, Scribe and Priest record the filled-in ranks with a note, so a later wiki edit shows up in `gwsim-extract diff`.

## Behaviour

Behaviour is in [T4.4.1](T4.4.1-foe-ai.md). In short, a group aggroes together, each foe picks the weakest reachable target, and casters use their bars by the same DSL-derived profile as heroes. Kiting is supported, but no Kournan foe is marked a kiter, since the pages describe none. Hard mode reacts faster (A-010).
