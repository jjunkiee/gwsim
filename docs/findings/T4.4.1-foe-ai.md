# T4.4.1 — Foe behaviour

**Task:** T4.4.1 (P4, WP4.4). **Status:** done. Implemented in `crates/gwsim-engine/src/ai/foe.rs` and the shared evaluator `ai/skills.rs`, and tested in `crates/gwsim-engine/tests/foe_ai.rs`.

## Sources

`research/` (Game mechanics, Creature types), and the wiki pages Aggro, Scatter, Area of effect, Hard mode, Monster skill and the eight Kournan foe pages, read through the extractor cache.

## Rules

| Rule | What gwsim does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AI-F1 aggro** | A group stands idle until a party creature comes within aggro range (A-009 = 1012, the aggro circle) of any member, or a member is attacked or damaged. Then the whole group engages together. A cast-time skill used on a foe also draws its group. | Aggro |
| **AI-F2 targeting** | A foe scores targets by low armor, low health and short distance, and sticks with its choice until the target dies. Spirits are a last resort (score × 0.5). | Aggro |
| **AI-F3 skills** | The same DSL-derived evaluator as heroes, aimed at the chosen target where a skill is offensive. Healers heal the most hurt ally, interrupters interrupt whatever is casting, and hexers hex the target. | Monster skill |
| **AI-F4 scatter** | Foes scatter from area damage and damage over time (A-016, still `Pending`). **Not implemented**: M1's foes face no damage-over-time area (F4.14). | Scatter |
| **AI-F5 movement** | Casters close to casting range and stand to cast. Melee foes chase. Stationary foes never move. Kiters step back when a melee attacker is within 200. | Aggro |
| **AI-F6 hard mode** | Foes react faster (A-010: 250 ms normal, 150 ms hard). Target choice gets a small random spread (up to +0.25 on the score) so foes ball up less. | Hard mode ("improved AI") |
| **AI-F8 kiting** | Supported as a per-unit flag. No Kournan page describes kiting, so no M1 foe kites. | — |

## Proposed values for pending assumptions

- **A-010, foe reaction delay:** 250 ms in normal mode and 150 ms in hard mode. The Hard mode page says foes have "improved AI" but gives no figure. 250 ms is the human delay's order of magnitude (A-012), and hard mode takes 40% off it so that the improvement is visible in results. **Set, `Assumed`.**
- **A-016, scatter trigger and timing:** left `Pending`. Nothing in M1 triggers a scatter, and the value needs the Scatter page's rules modelled first (F4.14, P7).

## Per-skill foe usage

The Monster skill page and the foe pages give no per-skill usage rule for any Kournan skill. General caveats such as "Backfire only on caster-weapon holders" and the Blind rules concern skills no Kournan foe carries. The Kournan bars are used by the general evaluator:

- the Priest's heals and Zealous Benediction on hurt allies;
- the Seer's Power Spike on casting party members;
- the Oppressor's Strip Enchantment and the Seer's Drain Enchantment and Shatter Enchantment on enchanted party members;
- the Scribe's Fireball, Meteor and Aftershock on its target;
- the martial foes' attack skills on their target.
